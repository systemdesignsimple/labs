Retrying a failed request can help when the failure is short and random. Retrying at once can make things worse when the server is already overloaded. Exponential backoff doubles the wait after each failed attempt, for example 100 ms, then 200 ms, then 400 ms. Adding random jitter spreads clients out so they don't all retry at the same moment. A retry budget caps retries at a small share of traffic, such as 10 percent. Without a cap, retries can multiply the load on a struggling service.
