Before two machines exchange data over TCP, they perform a three step handshake. The client sends a SYN packet to start the connection. The server answers with a SYN-ACK packet to accept it. The client then sends an ACK, and the connection is open. This setup costs one full round trip before any request data can move. On a link with 100 ms of round trip time, that is 100 ms of waiting on every new connection.
