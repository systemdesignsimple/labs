A content delivery network keeps copies of files on servers close to users. When a user asks for a file, the nearest edge server answers if it has a copy. If the edge does not have the file, it fetches it from the origin and stores it. A cache hit ratio of 90 percent means the origin serves only 1 request in 10. Purging a file tells every edge to drop its copy and fetch a new one. Purges can take from a few seconds to several minutes to reach every edge.
