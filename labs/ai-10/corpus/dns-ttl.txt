DNS turns a name like example.com into an address a computer can connect to. Each DNS record comes with a TTL, which is a number of seconds. A resolver may keep the answer in its cache until the TTL runs out. With a TTL of 300, a changed address can take up to 5 minutes to reach most users. Some resolvers keep answers longer than the TTL, so a change can take even more time. Lowering the TTL a day before a planned move makes the switch faster.
