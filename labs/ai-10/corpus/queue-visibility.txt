A message queue lets one service hand work to another without waiting for it. When a worker receives a message, the queue hides it from other workers for a visibility timeout. If the worker finishes and deletes the message, the work is done. If the worker crashes, the message becomes visible again after the timeout, such as 30 seconds. Another worker can then pick it up, so the same message may be processed twice. Workers should therefore make their processing safe to repeat.
