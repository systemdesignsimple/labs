A token bucket is a simple way to limit how many requests a client can make. The bucket holds up to 10 tokens and gains 1 new token every second. Each request takes one token out of the bucket. When the bucket is empty, the request is rejected or asked to wait. A client that has been quiet can send a short burst of up to 10 requests at once. Over a long period the client can't average more than 1 request per second.
